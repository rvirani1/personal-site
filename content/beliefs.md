---
title: Things I believe
---

1. **You’re going to die soon, so enjoy yourself and do your work.**
2. **Accept your battles. You need them.**
3. **When everyone cuts corners, integrity pays.**
4. **Owning your choices is the highest virtue.**
5. **Don’t fix your weaknesses. Enhance your strengths.**
6. **Hard work is less taxing than a leisurely existential crisis.**
7. **If something scares you, do it over and over again.**
8. **Read.**
9. **Bored people waste their own time. They’ll waste yours.**
10. **Talent density beats talent mass.**

---

I’ve been asked about these “Riaz-isms” many times. I almost always recommend one book: [The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/almanack-of-naval-ravikant). I’ve rarely found a text that so directly reflects my learnings in life. Read it.
